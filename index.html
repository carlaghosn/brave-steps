How this works. Anxiety shrinks when you face things in small steps and stay long enough for the feeling to come down, instead of escaping. Each time you do, your brain learns "I can handle this." Start small. Repeat a step until it feels easy, then move up.

Bravery ladders

Each ladder goes from easiest to hardest. Before a step, guess how anxious you'll be (0–10). After, rate how it really was. Most of the time, it's lower than you guessed.

The 10 thinking traps

Slow breathing

In for 4, out for 6. Longer breaths out calm your body down. Try 2 minutes.

ready

5-4-3-2-1 grounding

Notice 5 things you can see, 4 you can feel, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell, 1 you can taste. It pulls your attention out of the worry and into the room.

Cold water reset

When a feeling is too big to think straight: splash cold water on your face or hold something cold for 30 seconds while breathing out slowly. It quickly lowers your body's alarm.

Name it to tame it

Say to yourself: "I'm feeling anxious. It's a feeling, it will pass, and I don't have to act on it." Naming a feeling makes it smaller.
